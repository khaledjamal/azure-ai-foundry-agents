USER: How do I calculate my BMI, and what does it mean?

ASSISTANT: To calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI), you can use the following formula:

**BMI = weight (kg) / (height (m))²**

If you prefer to use pounds and inches, the formula is:

**BMI = (weight (lbs) / (height (inches))²) × 703**

Here’s a step-by-step guide:

1. Measure your weight in kilograms (or pounds).
2. Measure your height in meters (or inches).
3. Plug your measurements into one of the formulas above to calculate your BMI.

### What Does BMI Mean?

- **Underweight:** BMI less than 18.5
- **Normal weight:** BMI 18.5 to 24.9
- **Overweight:** BMI 25 to 29.9
- **Obesity:** BMI 30 or greater

### Important Considerations
- BMI is a useful screening tool but does not directly measure body fat or health.
- It may not be fully accurate for certain individuals, such as athletes with high muscle mass or older adults with low muscle mass.
