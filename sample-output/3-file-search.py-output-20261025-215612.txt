### 1. Baked Salmon
**Ingredients:**
- Salmon fillet
- Lemon slices
- Fresh herbs (like dill or parsley)

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Place the salmon on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
3. Season with salt, pepper, and top with lemon slices and herbs.
4. Bake for about 15-20 minutes or until the salmon is cooked through.

### 2. Mediterranean Bowl
**Ingredients:**
- Canned chickpeas (rinsed and drained)
- Mixed vegetables (e.g., cucumber, tomatoes, bell peppers)
- Tahini sauce or dressing

**Instructions:**
1. In a bowl, combine the chickpeas and chopped vegetables.
2. Drizzle with tahini sauce and mix well.
3. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
