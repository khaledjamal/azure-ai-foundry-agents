April in Kyoto means cherry blossoms and mild weather (10–20°C / 50–68°F). **Hidden Gem** – **Sengen Shrine** (just north of Kiyomizu-dera, tiny shrine with a small bamboo grove and no tourists).  
**Evening** – Stroll through **Gion** district (avoid main Hanami-koji street at peak hours; explore the quieter Shirakawa Canal area).  
**Safety** – Keep bags zipped in crowded Gion; respect geisha/miko by not touching or blocking their path.

---

## Day 2: Northern Kyoto – Temples & Mountain Escape

**Morning** – **Kinkaku-ji** (Golden Pavilion) early (opens 9am, arrive by 8:45 to beat buses).

**Lunch** – **Kuruma** (local soba near Ryoan-ji).  
**Afternoon** – **Kurama & Kibune** (30 min train from Kyoto Station). Hike the mountain path between the two villages – cherry blossoms along the river, hot spring footbath at Kurama Onsen.  
**Safety** – Wear sturdy shoes for the hike; trails can be slippery after rain. Bring water – limited vending machines on trail.

---

**Lunch** – **Omen** (udon near Ginkaku-ji).  
**Afternoon** – **Kiyomizu-dera** (go after 3pm – sunset views, fewer crowds). Then walk down **Ninenzaka** and **Sannenzaka** slopes (historic preserved streets).  
**Craft** – **Kyoto Handicraft Center** (try yuzen dyeing or pottery).  
**Safety** – Ninenzaka steps are uneven; watch your step. Keep an eye on kids in crowded temple areas.
