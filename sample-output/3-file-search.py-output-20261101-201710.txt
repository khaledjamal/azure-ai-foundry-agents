### Quinoa Bowl
**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup quinoa
- 2 cups water or vegetable broth
- Mixed vegetables (e.g., bell peppers, carrots, spinach)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: feta cheese, avocado, or grilled chicken for added protein

**Instructions:**
1. Rinse the quinoa under cold water.
2. In a pot, combine the quinoa and water (or vegetable broth). Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and cover.
3. Cook for about 15 minutes or until the liquid is absorbed. Remove from heat and let it sit covered for 5 minutes.
4. While the quinoa is cooking, sauté your mixed vegetables in olive oil over medium heat until tender.
5. Fluff the quinoa with a fork, then mix in the sautéed vegetables. Season with salt and pepper.
6. Optional: Top with feta cheese, sliced avocado, or grilled chicken for more flavor and nutrition.

Enjoy your healthy gluten-free lunch!
