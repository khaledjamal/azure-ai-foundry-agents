HIIT is perfect for building endurance and toning muscles quickly. It alternates bursts of intense activity with short rest periods. Here's a sample routine:
- **Warm-up**: 2 minutes of dynamic stretches (arm circles, leg swings).
- **Circuit** (repeat 3–4 times):
  - 40 seconds: Squats (add a jump for extra intensity).
  - 20 seconds: Rest.
  - 40 seconds: Push-ups (modify on knees if needed).
  - 20 seconds: Rest.
  - 40 seconds: Mountain climbers.
  - 20 seconds: Rest.
- **Cool-down**: 2–3 minutes of stretching (focus on quads, hamstrings, and shoulders).

### 2. **Bodyweight Strength Training - 20 minutes**
Strength training builds muscle tone without needing equipment. Here’s a quick plan:
- **Warm-up**: 2 minutes of light cardio (march in place or do jumping jacks).
- **Workout** (repeat 3 rounds):
  - 12–15 reps: Squats or lunges.
  - 12–15 reps: Push-ups or tricep dips (use a sturdy chair).
  - 12–15 reps: Plank shoulder taps (hold a plank position and alternate tapping each shoulder).
  - 30–60 seconds: Side plank (each side).
- **Cool-down**: Stretch the major muscle groups.

### 3. **Tabata Style - 10 minutes**
If you’re *really* pressed for time, Tabata workouts are super-efficient. They consist of 20 seconds of work followed by 10 seconds of rest for 8 rounds (4 minutes per exercise). Choose two exercises:
- Example 1: Burpees + Plank hold.
- Example 2: Jump squats + Push-ups.

### 4. **Core & Endurance Combo - 15 minutes**
A strong core is key for overall fitness and endurance. Try:
- 1 minute: Plank hold (modify on forearms or knees if needed).
- 30 seconds: Russian twists (with or without weights).
- 1 minute: Bicycle crunches.
- 30 seconds: Rest.
- Repeat 3 times.

### Tips for Staying Consistent:
- **Schedule it**: Treat your workout like an important meeting and block time in your calendar.
- **Keep it simple**: You don’t need fancy equipment—your body is the best tool.
- **Sneak it in**: If you’re super busy, break it into smaller chunks—10 minutes in the morning and 10 minutes in the evening.
